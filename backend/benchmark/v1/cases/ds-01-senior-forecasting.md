Fairmarch Grocery is recruiting a Senior Data Scientist for the supply chain team.

We move perishable goods through 1,200 stores. A forecast that is wrong in one direction costs us waste, and wrong in the other costs us empty shelves and a customer who shops elsewhere next week. The models you build will be checked against reality every single morning.

You will work alongside supply chain planners who have done this for twenty years and who will tell you, politely, when your model is wrong. The best data scientists we have hired treated that as the most valuable feedback loop in the building.

We are looking for:
- Five or more years of applied data science in industry, not only in research.
- Demand forecasting or time-series modelling at a scale where the infrastructure mattered.
- Strong SQL and Python. Our warehouse is the primary interface to everything.
- Experience designing and running controlled experiments, including knowing when not to.
- The ability to present model results to executives who will make a commercial decision on them.

Familiarity with causal inference is welcome but not required.
