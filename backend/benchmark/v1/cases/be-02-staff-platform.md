Northgate Systems is looking for a Staff Platform Engineer to lead the internal developer platform.

This is a role for someone who has done the job before. We have sixty engineers across nine teams, a Kubernetes estate that grew organically, and a deploy pipeline nobody is happy with. We are not asking you to rewrite it. We are asking you to decide what to keep.

The remit:
- Set the technical direction for the platform group and hold it across teams that do not report to you.
- Lead the migration off our current CI system, which will take three quarters and touch every team.
- Own the Terraform modules and the Kubernetes operators the platform exposes.
- Grow senior engineers into staff engineers. We will judge you partly on who is better because of you.

Requirements:
- Eight or more years in backend or platform engineering.
- A track record leading technical migrations across multiple teams, with the political work that implies.
- Deep Kubernetes and Terraform, at the level where you have debugged the control plane rather than only used it.
- Experience designing internal developer platforms that engineers chose to adopt.
- Formal mentorship or technical leadership of senior engineers.
