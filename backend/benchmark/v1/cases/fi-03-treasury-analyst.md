Kelvin Freight Group is looking for a Treasury Analyst.

We move freight across fourteen countries and invoice in nine currencies, so treasury is not a back-office function here — it is where a meaningful share of the margin is won or lost. The team is four people and reports to the Group CFO.

The role covers daily cash positioning, the rolling thirteen-week forecast, FX exposure reporting, and the operational side of our hedging programme.

We are looking for:
- Two or more years in treasury, banking or corporate finance.
- Cash flow forecasting, including the discipline of explaining last week's variance before producing next week's number.
- An understanding of FX exposure and the instruments used to hedge it. You do not need to have executed trades, but you need to know what a forward is and what it does not protect you from.
- Strong Excel.
- Experience with a treasury management system is an advantage; we run Kyriba.
