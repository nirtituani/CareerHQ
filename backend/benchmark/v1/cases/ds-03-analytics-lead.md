Perrin Insurance is looking for an Analytics Lead to take ownership of our reporting layer.

Insurance analytics is unglamorous and consequential. The numbers you publish will be used to price policies and to answer the regulator. Reproducibility matters more here than novelty, and a model nobody can explain is a model we cannot use.

The team is three analysts today and will be five. You will spend a meaningful share of your time on other people's work rather than your own.

We are looking for:
- Experience leading a small analytics team, formally or otherwise.
- Advanced SQL. Our warehouse has 400 tables and a history of people adding the 401st.
- Data modelling with dbt or an equivalent transformation layer.
- Stakeholder management across underwriting, claims and finance, who will disagree with each other in front of you.
- Insurance or regulated financial services background is strongly preferred; we will consider adjacent regulated industries.
