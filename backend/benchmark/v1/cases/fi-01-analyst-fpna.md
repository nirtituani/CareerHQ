Astrel Manufacturing is hiring an FP&A Analyst to join the group finance team.

We make industrial components across four plants, and the finance team is the only group that sees all four at once. That is the interesting part of this job: within a year you will understand the business better than most people who work in it.

The role covers the monthly management pack, budget and forecast cycles, and the variance analysis that explains why a plant missed its number — which is usually more interesting than the number.

We are looking for:
- One to three years in a finance or accounting role.
- Advanced Excel, including building a model somebody else can audit. We will ask you to talk through one.
- Exposure to month-end close, even if you were not running it.
- A degree in finance, accounting, economics or a related discipline.
- Business-level English; the group reports in English and the plants do not.

Progression here is towards a business partnering role rather than towards technical accounting, and we would rather say so now.
