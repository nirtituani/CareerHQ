Ashvale University Hospital is recruiting a Senior Staff Nurse for our adult intensive care unit.

The unit has eighteen beds and takes both elective post-surgical admissions and unplanned admissions from the emergency department and the wards. We run a rotating shift pattern including nights, and we staff to a one-to-one ratio for ventilated patients.

We are looking for a nurse who is already confident in critical care and who wants the next step: coordinating a shift, supporting newly qualified colleagues, and contributing to how the unit works rather than only working in it.

Essential:
- Current registration with the nursing board and no restrictions on practice.
- At least three years of post-registration experience in adult critical care.
- Competence in ventilator management and haemodynamic monitoring, evidenced in practice.
- Experience precepting or mentoring junior colleagues.
- Willingness to work a rotating shift pattern including nights and weekends.

Desirable: a post-registration critical care qualification, and experience of quality improvement work at ward level.
