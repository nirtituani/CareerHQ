Halstead & Roe is recruiting an Audit Senior for our mid-market assurance practice.

Our clients are owner-managed businesses and mid-cap groups between 20 and 400 million in turnover. You will run fieldwork on site, supervise one to three junior colleagues, and be the person the client's financial controller actually speaks to.

This is a role for someone who has finished their qualification and wants to lead engagements rather than sections of them.

Essential:
- A qualified accountant: ACA, ACCA, or an equivalent recognised qualification. Part-qualified candidates cannot be considered for this level.
- At least three years in external audit at a practice firm.
- Experience leading fieldwork teams, including reviewing the work of others.
- Working knowledge of IFRS, and of the judgements that get argued about.
- Willingness to travel to client sites, typically two to three days a week in season.

We will support the move to manager within two years for the right person.
