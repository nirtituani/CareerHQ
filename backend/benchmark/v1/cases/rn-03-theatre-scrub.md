Larkhill Orthopaedic Centre is recruiting a Theatre Scrub Nurse for our elective joint replacement lists.

We do one thing and we do a lot of it: primary hip and knee arthroplasty, roughly 3,000 procedures a year across six theatres. The lists are predictable, the instrumentation is specialised, and the surgical teams are stable.

You will scrub for joint replacement lists, manage instrumentation and implant selection with the surgical team, and take part in the on-call rota for trauma cases.

Essential:
- Current nursing registration with no restrictions.
- Scrub experience in an operating theatre. This is a scrub role and we cannot train from zero.
- Orthopaedic specialty experience, ideally arthroplasty.
- Familiarity with surgical safety checklists and instrument count procedures.
- Availability for the on-call rota, approximately one weekend in five.

We fund the orthopaedic scrub course for the right candidate who is strong on everything else.
