Aperture Mobility is hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to own how our models reach production.

We have a research team that produces good models and a serving stack that makes them take four months to deploy. This role exists to close that gap. It is more engineering than modelling, and we would rather be honest about that in the advert than in month three.

You will:
- Own the model serving path, from a trained artefact to a versioned endpoint with a rollback.
- Build the evaluation harness that decides whether a candidate model actually replaces the incumbent.
- Work with researchers on making models servable, which occasionally means saying no.

Requirements:
- Three or more years shipping machine learning into production systems.
- Strong software engineering practice. You write tests, you review code, and you have opinions about both.
- Experience with model serving infrastructure — Triton, TorchServe, KServe, or something you built.
- PyTorch or TensorFlow at the level of debugging a graph, not only calling fit.
- Kubernetes, because that is where our serving stack lives.
