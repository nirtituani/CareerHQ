We are hiring a Backend Engineer to join the ledger team at Corvid Financial.

The ledger team owns the services that record every movement of money through the platform: authorisations, captures, refunds, chargebacks and end-of-day settlement. Correctness is not negotiable here, and neither is the audit trail — we would rather be slow and right.

You will own services end to end. That means the schema, the API, the deploy, the dashboards and the pager. We run Python and Go on Kubernetes, with PostgreSQL as the system of record and Redis in front of the read paths that need it.

What we are looking for:
- Four or more years building production backend services that other teams depend on.
- Strong PostgreSQL. You should be comfortable reading a query plan and explaining why an index is not being used.
- Python or Go. We will not test you on the one you have not used.
- Experience with payment, billing or ledger systems is a significant advantage.
- Container orchestration on Kubernetes.
- Willingness to carry an on-call pager on a shared rotation, roughly one week in six.

We care about how you handle the incident, not whether you cause one.
