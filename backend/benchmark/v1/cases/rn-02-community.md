Riverbend Community Trust is hiring Community Nurses to work across our northern district.

Community nursing is not hospital nursing performed elsewhere. You will make clinical decisions alone, in someone's living room, with no colleague down the corridor and sometimes no signal on your phone. The people who thrive here are the ones who find that a feature rather than a risk.

A typical caseload is eight to eleven visits a day: wound care, catheter management, palliative support, and the long-term condition reviews that keep people out of hospital.

Essential:
- Current nursing registration with no restrictions.
- At least two years of post-registration experience in any setting.
- A full driving licence and access to a car. The district covers 40 rural kilometres and there is no practical alternative.
- Experience of wound care and long-term condition management.
- Competence and confidence in lone working.

We provide a mobile device, indemnity, and a named clinical supervisor.
