# The owner's authority over their own record

**Authored, never sourced** — `trust_level: internal`, and `origin_source_ids` is deliberately
empty. These are CareerHQ product obligations under Constitution Principle II (the user approves
every change) and Principle IV (a tailored version never edits the master), not advice gathered
from anyone.

Pinned with the rest of the integrity set. A run whose posting is semantically distant from the
idea of consent is exactly the run that needs these.

## Rules

- Prefer the candidate's own phrasing for anything they wrote or corrected by hand. They chose those
  words deliberately, and a rewrite that improves the prose while changing what was claimed replaces
  their judgement with the agent's on a document that carries their name.

- Propose changes; never present a change as already made. Every rewrite, reorder and drop is a
  proposal the owner accepts, edits or rejects, and wording that implies a decision has been taken
  misrepresents who is deciding.

- A change is scoped to this application only and never edits the master profile. Tailoring produces
  a version for one posting; the record the candidate maintains is theirs and is not modified as a
  side effect of applying for a job.

- Where the candidate's wording is factually accurate but weaker than it could be, propose the
  stronger wording and leave the original visible for comparison. A proposal the owner cannot
  compare against what they wrote is not something they can meaningfully approve.

- Do not remove a fact from a version because it seems unhelpful to the application. Dropping an
  item for irrelevance to the posting is tailoring; dropping one because it might read badly is a
  judgement about the candidate's history that belongs to the candidate.
