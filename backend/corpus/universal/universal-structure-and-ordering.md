# Document structure and ordering

**`trust_level: institutional`, and the file exists because of it.** These rules derive from S-001,
the Israel Employment Service's guidance on CV structures — a national employment service under the
Ministry of Labor. Its structural advice is universal rather than Israeli, so it is tagged
`market: global`: most of S-001's content is *universal advice from an Israeli source*, and
relabelling it Israeli by origin would manufacture a distinction the evidence does not support.

Kept separate from the rubric-derived universal files because those are `trust_level: internal` and
metadata is per document — the split that the Israeli files needed for the same reason.

**Two rules were moved out on 2026-08-28**, to `universal-document-conventions`: section-order
stability, and the employer/title/date requirement. Neither is contributed by S-001, so both were
inheriting this file's institutional standing without evidence behind them. The four rules that
remain are each traceable to S-001's recorded contributions.

**No rule here states a page count.** The sources conflict on length and the conflict is
unresolved; a length rule would be an assertion this corpus cannot support.

## Rules

- Use a chronological structure, most recent first, unless the candidate's record gives a specific
  reason not to. A functional structure organised by skill rather than by role is less common and
  some readers distrust it, so it is a deliberate choice for an unusual history rather than a
  default.

- Where the candidate held several distinct roles at one employer, list them as separate entries
  rather than merging them under one title and date range. The progression is itself evidence, and
  merging hides a promotion inside what then reads as a single long posting.

- For a long career, concentrate detail on roughly the last decade to fifteen years and compress
  what came before into brief entries. Where earlier work is directly relevant to this posting,
  keep it in full rather than compressing it on age alone.

- Include volunteer work, temporary positions and youth-movement or community leadership as
  experience where they demonstrate something this posting asks for. Where they demonstrate nothing
  the posting asks for, they are candidates for dropping like any other item.
